Bhramari is a breathing practice that is commonly included in basic yoga routines. It is known for the gentle humming sound made during exhalation. Many people practice Bhramari while sitting comfortably and keeping the body relaxed. This practice is often introduced to beginners because it is simple and does not involve physical movement.

Regular practice of Bhramari helps create a sense of calm and steady breathing. It supports relaxation and helps the mind feel quieter. Many people feel that the humming sound makes it easier to focus during practice. Bhramari is often used at the end of a yoga session to slow down and settle the body after activity.

Students, office workers, and people with busy daily routines often include Bhramari in their practice. It is suitable for those who prefer gentle breathing exercises rather than intense physical poses. People who are new to yoga or looking for simple breathing practices usually find Bhramari easy to follow.

Bhramari should be practiced gently without forcing the breath or sound. If someone feels discomfort, pressure, or dizziness, it is better to stop and rest. People with ear discomfort or recent health concerns may choose to avoid this practice. It is important to stay relaxed and practice within personal comfort levels.
