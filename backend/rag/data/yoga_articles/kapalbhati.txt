Kapalbhati is a breathing practice that is commonly included in yoga routines. It involves short and active exhalations with a relaxed inhalation. Many people practice Kapalbhati while sitting comfortably and keeping the body steady. This practice is usually introduced after learning basic breathing awareness.

Regular practice of Kapalbhati helps improve breathing control and body awareness. It supports a feeling of lightness and energy in the body. Many people feel that it helps them stay active and focused during the day. Kapalbhati is often practiced as part of a morning yoga routine before physical postures.

Students and regular yoga practitioners often include Kapalbhati in their practice. It is usually chosen by people who are familiar with basic breathing exercises. Those who prefer gentle practices may practice it for a short duration and at a comfortable pace.

Kapalbhati should be practiced carefully and without strain. If someone feels dizziness, discomfort, or fatigue, it is better to stop and rest. People with breathing discomfort or recent health concerns may choose to avoid this practice. Practicing calmly and within personal limits is important.
