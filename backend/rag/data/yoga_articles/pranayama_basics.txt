Pranayama is a basic breathing practice that is commonly included in yoga routines. It focuses on becoming aware of the breath and breathing in a calm and controlled way. Many people learn pranayama as part of beginner yoga sessions. It is usually practiced while sitting comfortably and keeping the body relaxed.

Regular practice of pranayama helps improve breathing awareness and calmness. It supports relaxation and helps the mind feel more settled. Many people feel that paying attention to the breath makes it easier to stay focused during yoga practice. Pranayama is often practiced before or after yoga postures as a way to balance the body and mind.

Beginners, students, and people with busy daily routines often practice pranayama. It is suitable for those who prefer gentle practices rather than physical movement. People who are new to yoga usually find pranayama helpful for building a steady and mindful habit.

Pranayama should be practiced gently and without forcing the breath. If someone feels uncomfortable, dizzy, or restless, it is better to stop and rest. People with breathing discomfort may choose to keep the practice short. Practicing within personal comfort levels helps make pranayama a safe and positive experience.
