Stress relief yoga is a gentle form of yoga that focuses on calming the body and mind. It usually includes slow movements, relaxed breathing, and resting poses. Many people practice stress relief yoga to unwind after a busy or tiring day. The practice is calm and does not involve intense physical effort.

Regular practice of stress relief yoga helps the body relax and release tension. It supports steady breathing and encourages a slower pace. Many people feel that gentle stretching and quiet moments help them feel more balanced. Stress relief yoga is often practiced in the evening or during breaks.

Students, office workers, and people with busy schedules often choose stress relief yoga. It is suitable for those who feel mentally tired or overwhelmed. People who prefer slow and mindful practices usually find this type of yoga comfortable and easy to follow.

Stress relief yoga should be practiced gently and without pushing the body. Strong or demanding poses are usually avoided. If any movement feels uncomfortable, it is better to stop or rest. Practicing within personal comfort levels helps make stress relief yoga a calming and safe experience.
