Restorative yoga is a gentle form of yoga that focuses on rest and relaxation. It usually involves holding simple and comfortable positions with full support. Many people practice restorative yoga to slow down and relax the body. The practice is calm and does not involve active movement.

Regular practice of restorative yoga helps the body release tension and feel more relaxed. It supports rest and encourages steady breathing. Many people feel that this type of yoga helps them feel calmer after a busy day. Restorative yoga is often practiced in the evening or during quiet moments.

Students, beginners, and people who feel tired or stressed often choose restorative yoga. It is suitable for those who prefer slow and gentle practices. People who want to relax without physical effort usually find restorative yoga helpful and easy to practice.

Restorative yoga should be practiced comfortably and without strain. Poses should feel supportive rather than challenging. If any position causes discomfort, it is better to adjust or rest. Practicing within comfort limits helps make restorative yoga a safe and positive experience.
