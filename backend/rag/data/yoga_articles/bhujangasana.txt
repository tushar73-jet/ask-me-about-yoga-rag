Bhujangasana is a basic yoga posture that is commonly practiced in regular yoga routines. It involves gently lifting the upper body while keeping the lower body relaxed on the floor. Many people practice this pose slowly and with attention to breathing. It is often included in beginner-level yoga sessions because the movement is simple and controlled.

Regular practice of Bhujangasana helps improve body awareness and posture. It supports gentle stretching of the chest and front of the body. Many people feel that this pose helps them feel more open and relaxed after sitting for long periods. It is often practiced as part of a sequence that includes other floor-based yoga poses.

Beginners and regular yoga learners both practice Bhujangasana. It is commonly included in routines focused on flexibility and general fitness. People who prefer slow and mindful yoga practices often find this pose suitable for daily use. It is also practiced by those looking to maintain an active lifestyle in a gentle way.

Bhujangasana should be practiced carefully and without forcing the back. People with back discomfort or recent strain may choose to avoid this pose. If any pain or strong discomfort is felt, it is better to stop and rest. Listening to the body and practicing within comfort limits is important.
