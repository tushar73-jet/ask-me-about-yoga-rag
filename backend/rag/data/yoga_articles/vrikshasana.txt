Vrikshasana is a standing yoga posture that focuses on balance and steadiness. It involves standing on one leg while maintaining an upright posture. Many people practice this pose slowly with attention to breathing. Vrikshasana is often included in yoga sessions that focus on balance and body awareness.

Regular practice of Vrikshasana helps improve balance and concentration. It supports posture and encourages calm breathing while standing still. Many people feel that this pose helps them feel more grounded and focused. Vrikshasana is often practiced along with other standing poses.

Beginners and regular yoga learners both practice Vrikshasana. It is suitable for people who want to improve balance in a gentle way. Those who prefer slow and mindful postures often include this pose in their routine. It is commonly practiced in beginner-level yoga classes.

Vrikshasana should be practiced carefully and without forcing balance. People with balance difficulties may choose to practice near support. If any discomfort or strain is felt, it is better to come out of the pose and rest. Practicing within comfort limits helps make Vrikshasana a safe and positive experience.
