Yoga and sleep are often connected in daily wellness routines. Many people practice gentle yoga to help the body relax before resting. Yoga for sleep usually focuses on slow movements, calm breathing, and quiet moments. It is often practiced in the evening or close to bedtime.

Regular practice of yoga for sleep helps the body slow down and feel more relaxed. It supports calm breathing and helps release tension built up during the day. Many people feel that gentle stretching and resting poses make it easier to unwind. Yoga is often used as part of a bedtime routine to prepare the body for rest.

Students, office workers, and people with busy schedules often include yoga in their evening routine. It is suitable for those who feel restless at night or find it hard to relax before sleep. People who prefer calm and gentle activities usually find yoga helpful for winding down.

Yoga for sleep should be practiced gently and without physical strain. Strong or active poses are usually avoided at night. If any movement feels uncomfortable, it is better to stop or rest. Practicing within comfort limits helps make yoga a calming and positive experience before sleep.
