Anulom Vilom is a simple breathing practice that is commonly included in daily yoga routines. It involves slow and controlled breathing through one nostril at a time. Many people practice it while sitting comfortably in a quiet place. This breathing method is often taught to beginners because it is easy to learn and does not require physical movement.

Regular practice of Anulom Vilom helps improve breathing awareness and calmness. It supports relaxation and helps the body feel more balanced. Many people feel that it helps them slow down their thoughts and stay focused. It is often used as part of a morning or evening yoga routine to create a sense of mental clarity and steady breathing.

Students, office workers, and people with busy schedules often include Anulom Vilom in their daily practice. It is suitable for those who prefer gentle yoga practices rather than intense physical exercises. People who want to build a habit of mindful breathing usually find this practice helpful and easy to maintain.

Anulom Vilom should be practiced gently and without holding the breath forcefully. If someone feels dizzy or uncomfortable, it is better to stop and rest. People with breathing discomfort or recent health concerns may choose to avoid this practice. It is important to practice calmly and within personal comfort levels.
