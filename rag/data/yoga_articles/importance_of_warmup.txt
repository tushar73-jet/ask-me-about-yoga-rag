Warm-up is an important part of a yoga practice. It is usually done before starting main postures or movements. Warm-up involves gentle and slow actions that prepare the body. Many people include warm-up at the beginning of a yoga session to feel more comfortable during practice.

Regular warm-up helps the body feel ready for movement. It supports better flexibility and makes muscles feel less stiff. Many people notice that starting slowly helps them move with more ease during yoga. Warm-up also helps build awareness of the body and breathing before deeper practice.

Beginners and regular yoga learners both benefit from warm-up. People who sit for long hours or feel stiffness often find warm-up especially helpful. It is also useful for those returning to yoga after a break, as it helps the body adjust gradually.

Warm-up should be done gently and without rushing. Sudden or forceful movements are usually avoided during this stage. If any movement feels uncomfortable, it is better to slow down or stop. Paying attention to comfort and body signals helps make yoga practice safer and more enjoyable.
