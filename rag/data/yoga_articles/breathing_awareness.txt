Breathing awareness is a simple practice often included in yoga and relaxation routines. It involves paying attention to the natural flow of breath without trying to change it. Many people practice breathing awareness while sitting or lying down in a comfortable position. This practice is easy to start and does not require any special technique.

Regular practice of breathing awareness helps people become more present and calm. It supports relaxation and helps the mind slow down. Many people feel that noticing the breath makes it easier to stay focused during yoga or daily activities. Breathing awareness is often used at the beginning of a yoga session to prepare the body and mind.

Students, beginners, and people with busy schedules often practice breathing awareness. It is suitable for those who prefer gentle and quiet practices rather than physical movement. People who are new to yoga usually find this practice helpful for building a basic connection with their breath.

Breathing awareness should be practiced comfortably and without forcing the breath. If someone feels restless or uncomfortable, it is okay to pause or change position. People with breathing discomfort may choose to keep the practice short. The focus should remain gentle and within personal comfort levels.
