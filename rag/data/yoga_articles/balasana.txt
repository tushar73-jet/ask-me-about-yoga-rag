Balasana, also known as the resting pose, is a gentle yoga posture commonly used during yoga practice. It involves sitting back and folding the body forward in a relaxed way. Many people use this pose as a break between more active movements. It is usually practiced slowly and with calm breathing.

Regular practice of Balasana helps the body relax and release tension. It supports gentle stretching of the back and hips and encourages steady breathing. Many people feel that this pose helps them feel calmer after physical activity. It is often used at the beginning or end of a yoga session to allow the body to settle.

Beginners and experienced yoga learners both practice Balasana. It is especially common among people who prefer slow and mindful yoga practices. Those who feel tired during practice often use this pose to rest without stopping their session completely. It is also included in routines focused on relaxation and body awareness.

Balasana should be practiced comfortably and without forcing the body into the pose. People with knee discomfort or difficulty bending forward may choose to avoid it. If any strain or discomfort is felt, it is better to come out of the pose gently. Listening to the body is important while practicing this posture.
