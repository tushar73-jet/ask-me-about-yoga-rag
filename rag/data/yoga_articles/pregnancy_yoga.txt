Pregnancy yoga refers to gentle yoga practices that are adapted for comfort and relaxation during pregnancy. It usually focuses on slow movements, simple breathing, and resting poses. Many people practice pregnancy yoga to stay active in a calm and mindful way. The practice is generally softer than regular yoga routines.

Regular pregnancy yoga helps improve body awareness and relaxation. It supports gentle movement and helps release everyday tension. Many people feel that slow stretching and calm breathing make the body feel lighter and more comfortable. Pregnancy yoga is often practiced to create a sense of balance and calmness during daily life.

Pregnancy yoga is commonly practiced by people who want gentle activity during pregnancy. It is usually chosen by those who prefer slow and careful movement rather than strong physical exercise. People who already have some familiarity with yoga often find it easier to follow adapted practices.

Pregnancy yoga should always be practiced gently and without strain. Strong poses, deep twists, or forceful breathing are usually avoided. If any discomfort, pain, or unusual feeling is noticed, it is better to stop and rest. Listening to the body and staying within comfort limits is very important during pregnancy.
