Tadasana is a basic standing yoga posture that is commonly practiced at the beginning of yoga sessions. It involves standing still with attention to posture and balance. Many people practice this pose to become aware of body alignment and breathing. Tadasana is often used as a foundation for other standing poses.

Regular practice of Tadasana helps improve body awareness and posture. It supports balance and encourages steady breathing. Many people feel that standing calmly in this pose helps them feel more grounded and focused. Tadasana is often practiced slowly and with full attention.

Beginners and regular yoga learners both practice Tadasana. It is suitable for people of all ages because it does not require strong physical effort. Those who want to improve posture or build a calm start to their practice often include this pose in their routine.

Tadasana should be practiced comfortably and without stiffness. If standing for long feels tiring, it is okay to rest. People with balance difficulties may choose to practice near support. Practicing within comfort limits helps make Tadasana a safe and positive experience.
