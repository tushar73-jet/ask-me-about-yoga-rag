Shavasana is a resting yoga posture commonly practiced at the end of a yoga session. It involves lying down comfortably and allowing the body to relax completely. Many people use this pose to rest after physical movement. Shavasana is usually practiced in a quiet and calm environment.

Regular practice of Shavasana helps the body relax and settle after activity. It supports stillness and encourages steady breathing. Many people feel that this pose helps them feel calmer and more refreshed. Shavasana is often used to bring a sense of balance at the end of yoga practice.

Beginners and regular yoga learners both practice Shavasana. It is suitable for people of all ages because it does not involve physical effort. Those who feel tired after practice often find this pose helpful for resting without movement.

Shavasana should be practiced comfortably and without forcing stillness. If lying flat feels uncomfortable, small adjustments can be made. People who feel restless may keep the practice short. Practicing within comfort limits helps make Shavasana a peaceful and positive experience.
